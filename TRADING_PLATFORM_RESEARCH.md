# FEATURE GAPS IDENTIFIED ACROSS ALL PLATFORMS

1. **No single platform combines ALL features** - traders use 2-3 platforms simultaneously
2. **Backtesting quality varies wildly** - TradingView is basic, MT5/NinjaTrader/QuantConnect are advanced
3. **AI features are nascent** - mostly bolt-on rather than core
4. **Cross-platform sync** is weak - most platforms don't sync workspaces across devices
5. **Options analytics** are siloed - only thinkorswim and TWS do it well
6. **Social/community** is underdeveloped outside TradingView
7. **Risk management** is fragmented - most platforms have basic SL/TP, few have portfolio-level risk
8. **No-code strategy building** exists but is limited - NinjaTrader's Strategy Builder is best
9. **Alert scheduling** is rare - only TradingView (2026) offers true scheduling
10. **Developer ecosystem** lock-in - each platform has its own language (Pine, MQL5, NinjaScript, thinkScript)
